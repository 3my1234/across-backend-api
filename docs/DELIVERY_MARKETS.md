# Buyer delivery markets

Products now distinguish stock location from where the seller offers delivery. A local-stock product may serve only its stock country. An imported or cross-border product may serve any enabled buyer market for which the seller enters a complete delivered price. State and city entries narrow a country offer; the most specific matching entry sets the checkout price. Sellers must include their delivery charges and any duties or taxes they charge the buyer in that price. Atlantic Express adds its disclosed marketplace service fee.

Country, not region, is the pricing and payment unit. Names such as ATL Africa, ATL Europe, ATL US, and ATL Asia may group storefront navigation, but they must never determine a shared region-wide currency or convert prices automatically. A buyer sees only offers for the selected delivery country and address; the market currency and seller's matching delivered price are used again by the server when it creates the order and Flutterwave checkout. The order preserves that amount and currency. On the mobile app, changing the delivery country or address discards any pending quote so the buyer reviews the new price before paying.

Migration `054_product_delivery_areas.sql` creates the delivery-area table and preserves only the existing Nigerian-market offers. It intentionally does not expose existing local stock outside its stock country. Deploy the migration before the backend image that queries `product_delivery_areas`; otherwise catalog and checkout requests will fail. Existing Nigerian offers are backfilled only when their price is positive and denominated in NGN.

`countries_config` is the buyer-market allowlist. `/api/v1/buyer-markets` returns active Flutterwave markets and their checkout currencies. The mobile app offers those markets for account delivery country selection; sellers can enter delivery areas only for enabled markets and must use that market's currency. Checkout validates the saved buyer country, complete address and seller's matching area. No automatic FX conversion or shipping/duty calculation is performed.

Additional countries are deliberately not enabled by migration 054. Before activating one, verify this Flutterwave merchant account can collect in its currency and that the actual seller subaccount can receive the intended split and settlement. Then create or update its `countries_config` row with the country's ISO two-letter code, ISO three-letter currency, `is_active=true`, and `flutterwave` in `active_payment_gateways`. Test a seller offer, buyer quote, checkout and settlement in that market before public promotion. The old `ALLOWED_COUNTRIES` IP gate no longer controls buyer authentication or checkout; account delivery country and active `countries_config` rows do.

The current loyalty copy and purchase XP tiers are NGN-based, and the paid provider subscription plan is NGN-based. These need a separate country-specific product decision before claiming a complete worldwide commercial launch. Existing Flutterwave recurring subscriptions and their billing terms are unaffected by delivery-market changes.
